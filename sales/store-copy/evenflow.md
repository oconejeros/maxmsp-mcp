## Title

**EVENFLOW — Well-Formed rhythm generator for Ableton Live**

## Hook (2 lines)

Rhythms built the way scales are — from a single recurring interval — so every pattern sits
evenly in the bar and morphs cleanly into the next. Move one knob and the groove reshapes
itself, always balanced.

## What it does

- **Well-formed / moment-of-symmetry engine** (same family of ideas as XronoMorph):
  rhythmic patterns with two note lengths distributed as evenly as possible.
- **r-marker catalogue**: walk a space of rhythmic generators — every point is a distinct,
  ordered pattern, not a random one.
- **Morph A/B**: pin two patterns to two slots and cross-fade between them with one knob;
  the in-between states stay valid.
- **20 preset slots** saved to disk.
- **Quantize R**: locks the generator to clean subdivisions when you want it straight.
- Outputs MIDI: drives any instrument or Drum Rack.

## Requirements

- Ableton Live 12 or later with **Max for Live**.
- MIDI-effect device.

Well-formed and moment-of-symmetry rhythms — the time-domain translation of MOS scales.

## FAQ

- **Is it like XronoMorph?** It shares the well-formed-pattern idea but lives inside Live
  and outputs MIDI to your instruments.
- **Does it do "normal" 4/4 rhythms?** Yes — Quantize R snaps it to the grid; without it you
  explore less obvious meters.
