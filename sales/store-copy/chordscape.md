## Title

**Chordscape — Navigate the space of every chord, for Ableton Live**

## Hook

Every chord is a point. Every voice-leading move is a step to a neighbour. Chordscape
draws that whole space as a ring you click through — and every stop along the way plays.

## What it does

- **Two exploration modes**: **Nearest** walks the entire universe of 4,095 concrete
  chords (every transposition of all 351 set classes), ranked by how little the voices
  have to move to get there. **Steps** confines you to one chosen scale and moves one
  voice at a time — shift a voice a scale-step, drop one, or add one — so you can wander
  between chords of different sizes without ever leaving the scale.
- **Two views of the same space**: **Rings** — concentric rings of reachable neighbours,
  click any node to jump (and hear it). **Spiral** — a single continuous winding curve
  through the scale, with the reachable one-voice-move neighbours as clickable ghost
  points on the same curve.
- **Scalar transposition**: shift every voice one scale-step at once, keeping the chord's
  shape intact — a different move from single-voice navigation.
- **Register control**: notes are voiced across an adjustable spread (not glued to one
  octave), with quick octave-nudge buttons.

## Requirements

- Ableton Live 12 or later with **Max for Live**.

## Theory background

Built on generalized voice-leading-space geometry (Callender/Quinn/Tymoczko, *A Geometry
of Music*) — the same math behind sites like madmusicalscience.com's chord-space
explorers, reimplemented inside Live so every click plays through your own instruments.
