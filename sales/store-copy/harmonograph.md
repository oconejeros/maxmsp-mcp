## Title

**Harmonograph — Perfectly-Balanced pendulum rhythms for Ableton Live**

## Hook (2 lines)

Eight rhythmic layers, each one a spinning pendulum. Watch them draw a Lissajous curve as
they play — the picture IS the rhythm, and dragging it changes what you hear.

## What it does

- **Perfectly-Balanced (PB) rhythm engine**: each layer is a regular K-gon rotating at its
  own rate — frequency = sides, phase = rotation, amplitude = weight. Stack up to
  **8 layers** and their onsets merge into one polyrhythm that's always balanced.
- **The curve is the interface**: a live Lissajous/harmonograph portrait of the rhythm
  stack, with a phosphor comet tracing the current cycle and a flashing dot per onset.
- **Drag ring**: grab any layer's handle on the curve to change its rotation and weight by
  ear — no menus, the picture updates live.
- **Probability**: per-layer and global chance-to-sound, so the pattern breathes instead of
  repeating identically forever.
- **Two quantized modulation lanes** (Rotation, Weight) — MiniSteps-style: draw an 8-step
  shape that nudges every layer's rotation/weight once per cycle.
- **20 in-device preset slots** with named picker, Save/Load/Clear — your patterns are
  part of the device, not buried in Live's own preset browser.
- Syncs to Live's transport/tempo, or runs free at its own Period.

## Requirements

- Ableton Live 12 or later with **Max for Live**.
- MIDI-effect device: drives any instrument or Drum Rack.

## Theory background

Perfectly-Balanced rhythms (the union of regular polygons on one grid always balances) and
the harmonograph/Lissajous tradition of drawing rhythm as a physical curve. No theory
needed to use it — turn a knob, watch the shape, listen.

## FAQ

- **Is this like EVENFLOW?** Same rhythm-generation family (both come out of the
  Well-Formed / Perfectly-Balanced research), different engine and a completely different
  interface — EVENFLOW morphs between two saved patterns, Harmonograph stacks spinning
  layers you watch and drag.
- **Do I need to understand the math?** No — the curve is a manipulation surface, not a
  reading exercise.
- **Does Lite expire?** No.
- **Discount if I own FORTESEQ/EVENFLOW?** Yes, buyers get a coupon at launch.
